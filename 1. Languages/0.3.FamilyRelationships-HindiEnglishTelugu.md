Key Cultural Nuance in Telugu

* **Parallel vs. Cross Cousins:** In Telugu, children of same-sex siblings (father's brothers or mother's sisters) are considered equivalent to siblings and are called **Annayya / Tammudu** (brother) and **Akka / Chellelu** (sister). Children of opposite-sex siblings (mother's brothers or father's sisters) are cross-cousins and use the same terms as brothers-in-law and sisters-in-law (**Bava / Bavamaridi** and **Vadina / Maradalu**).

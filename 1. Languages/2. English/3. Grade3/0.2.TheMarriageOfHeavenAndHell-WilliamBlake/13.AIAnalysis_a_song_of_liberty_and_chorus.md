# Comprehensive Study Guide: Section 13 – A Song of Liberty and Chorus

**From William Blake’s *The Marriage of Heaven and Hell* (Plates 25–27)**

*(Note on Section 13: **"A Song of Liberty"** and its concluding **"Chorus"** form the grand apocalyptic finale of *The Marriage of Heaven and Hell*. Written in twenty numbered prophetic verses modeled on the cadence of the King James Bible, this section translates the book's philosophical and psychological "Marriage" of Reason and Energy onto the stage of world history and cosmic myth. Referencing the American Revolution \[1776\], the French Revolution and the storming of the Bastille \[1789\], the call for the abolition of slavery, and the overthrow of authoritarian empire, Blake dramatizes the birth of the fiery spirit of human freedom \[the **"Son of Fire,"** whom he later names **Orc**\] and his triumph over the tyrannical **"starry king"** of cold Reason and stony Law \[whom he later names **Urizen**\], culminating in the immortal declaration: **"For everything that lives is holy."**)*

---

## Verse-by-Verse and Sentence-by-Sentence Breakdown

### Verse 1

> **Original Text:**
> "1. The Eternal Female groan’d; it was heard over all the earth:"

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* 1. Mother Earth and collective Nature ("The Eternal Female") cried out in the fierce labor pains of childbirth, and her groan echoed across the entire globe:
  *Implied Meaning:* Alluding both to the "woman clothed with the sun" crying out in labor in Revelation 12:1–2 and to Saint Paul’s statement in Romans 8:22 (*"the whole creation groaneth and travaileth in pain together until now"*), Blake opens his revolutionary hymn with a cosmic birth. **"The Eternal Female"** represents living Nature and collective humanity groaning under centuries of political and religious oppression as she prepares to give birth to a new era of human liberty.

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  Blake starts this final song with a giant sound heard all around the whole planet! **"The Eternal Female"**—who represents Mother Nature and the whole family of Earth—lets out a deep, powerful groan, like a mother getting ready to give birth to a brand-new baby! A brand-new age of freedom is about to be born, and the whole world can hear it coming!

---

### Verse 2

> **Original Text:**
> "2. Albion’s coast is sick silent; the American meadows faint."

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* 2. The shores of Britain ("Albion’s coast") lie paralyzed in anxious, repressive silence, while across the ocean, the newly liberated fields of America ("the American meadows") grow exhausted from their recent revolutionary struggle.
  *Implied Meaning:* **"Albion"** is the ancient mythological and poetic name for Britain. In the early 1790s, the British government under King George III and Prime Minister William Pitt reacted with terrified repression ("sick silent") to the spread of democratic ideals, censoring the press and fearing revolution at home. Meanwhile, across the Atlantic, **"the American meadows"**—where the American Revolution had recently triumphed in 1776–1783—tremble with exhaustion and anticipation as the spark of liberty prepares to leap back across the ocean to Europe.

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  **"Albion"** is the old storybook name for England (where William Blake lived). Blake looks at the seashore of England and sees that everyone is quiet and nervous ("sick silent") because the king’s strict rules won't let people speak freely. Across the ocean, the green fields of **America** ("the American meadows") are tired and catching their breath after fighting so hard for their freedom!

---

### Verse 3

> **Original Text:**
> "3. Shadows of prophecy shiver along by the lakes and the rivers, and mutter across the ocean. France, rend down thy dungeon!"

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* 3. Prophetic premonitions of revolution ("Shadows of prophecy") tremble along the great lakes and rivers of North America and rumble across the Atlantic Ocean toward Europe, crying out: "O France, tear down your stone prison ('thy dungeon')!"
  *Implied Meaning:* Blake traces the historical wave of eighteenth-century revolution moving from West to East: born along the lakes and rivers of North America during the American Revolution, the prophetic spirit of liberty crosses the Atlantic Ocean and ignites the **French Revolution**! The command **"France, rend down thy dungeon!"** is a direct celebration of the **Storming of the Bastille** on July 14, 1789, when the citizens of Paris tore down the medieval fortress-prison ("dungeon") that symbolized absolute royal tyranny. (Remember also Proverb 21: *"Prisons are built with stones of law."*)

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  Whispers of exciting change ("Shadows of prophecy") shiver along the lakes and rivers of America and zoom all the way across the Atlantic Ocean to Europe!
  Then the voice shouts out to the country of **France**: **"France, tear down your giant stone prison ('rend down thy dungeon')!"**
  Right when Blake was writing this book (in 1789), the people of France really did march up to a giant, unfair royal prison in Paris called the **Bastille** and tore it down brick by brick so prisoners could be free!

---

### Verse 4

> **Original Text:**
> "4. Golden Spain, burst the barriers of old Rome!"

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* 4. And you, wealthy and sunlit Spain ("Golden Spain"), shatter the ecclesiastical chains and inquisitorial walls imposed upon you by ancient papal authority ("old Rome")!
  *Implied Meaning:* Having called upon France to overthrow political tyranny (the Bastille), Blake calls upon **"Golden Spain"**—then the stronghold of absolute monarchy and the Spanish Inquisition—to break free from the spiritual and ideological tyranny of **"old Rome"** (the authoritarian hierarchy of the Papacy and the old Roman Empire). Political liberation (France) must be matched by religious and intellectual liberation (Spain and Rome).

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  Next, the voice calls out to sunny **"Golden Spain"** and tells the people there to burst right through the strict walls and barriers of **"old Rome"**—meaning the ancient, bossy religious rules that kept people in Spain from thinking and speaking freely!

---

### Verse 5

> **Original Text:**
> "5. Cast thy keys, O Rome, into the deep—down falling, even to eternity down falling;"

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* 5. Throw your papal keys of authority, O Rome, down into the bottomless ocean—falling downward, sinking forever into eternity;
  *Implied Meaning:* In traditional Church doctrine (based on a literalistic reading of Matthew 16:19), the Pope in Rome holds the **"keys of the kingdom of heaven"**—claiming the exclusive institutional power to lock or unlock salvation and bind human consciences. Blake commands Rome to hurl those keys of spiritual gatekeeping into "the deep" (the ocean of eternity), so that no priest or institution can ever again claim the right to lock the doors of Heaven or Hell against humanity.

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  The rulers of old Rome used to carry symbol **keys**, claiming that *they* were the only ones who could lock or unlock the doors to Heaven! Blake tells Rome to **throw those bossy keys deep into the ocean**—falling down, down, down forever—because nobody should ever lock up people's minds or hearts again!

---

### Verse 6

> **Original Text:**
> "6. And weep!"

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* 6. And mourn the loss of your tyrannical empire!
  *Implied Meaning:* Echoing the fall of mystical Babylon in Revelation 18:9–19 (where the kings and merchants of the earth weep as the great empire of oppression sinks like a millstone into the sea), Blake commands the old order of religious and political tyranny to weep as its power vanishes forever.

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  And as those bossy keys sink to the bottom of the sea, the old bullies and rule-makers can only cry (**"And weep!"**) because their unfair power is gone!

---

### Verse 7

> **Original Text:**
> "7. In her trembling hands she took the new-born terror, howling."

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* 7. Returning to the cosmic birth from Verse 1: the Eternal Female lifted up in her trembling hands the newly born infant of revolutionary Energy—a "new-born terror" howling with fierce, untamed life.
  *Implied Meaning:* The groan of the Eternal Female in Verse 1 now culminates in the birth of a child: **"the new-born terror, howling."** In Blake’s emerging mythology (which he expands soon after in *America a Prophecy*), this fiery infant is named **Orc**—the spirit of passionate human Energy, Desire, and Revolution. Why is a newborn baby called a **"terror"** who is **"howling"**? Because to the old world of tyrants and "Angels," the birth of unbridled human freedom and truth is terrifying, and like every healthy newborn (and like Rintrah and the roaring lions), this child enters the world with a lusty, unashamed cry of life! (Contrast this vibrant newborn with Proverb 66: *"Sooner murder an infant in its cradle than nurse unacted desires"*—this infant is Desire acted upon!)

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  Remember in Verse 1 how Mother Earth ("The Eternal Female") was groaning to give birth? Now the baby is born! With trembling hands, she picks up the brand-new baby, who is crying out loud and strong (**"howling"**)!
  Why does Blake call the baby **"the new-born terror"**? Not because the baby is mean, but because this glowing baby is the **Spirit of Freedom and Creative Energy**—and to the grumpy, unfair kings, a brave new spirit of Freedom is the scariest thing they have ever seen!

---

### Verse 8

> **Original Text:**
> "8. On those infinite mountains of light now barr’d out by the Atlantic sea, the new-born fire stood before the starry king."

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* 8. High upon those boundless mountains of visionary light—the lost paradise of Atlantis that is now cut off and submerged beneath the barrier of the Atlantic Ocean—the newly born spirit of creative Fire stood face-to-face in confrontation before the cold monarch of the night sky ("the starry king").
  *Implied Meaning:* This single verse introduces two of the grandest symbols in Blake’s entire mythological universe:
  1. **"Those infinite mountains of light now barr’d out by the Atlantic sea":** Referring to the ancient myth of **Atlantis** (from Plato’s *Timaeus* and *Critias*), Blake imagines that before humanity fell into narrow five-senses materialism, golden mountains of infinite light connected Britain/Europe and America in unbroken visionary unity. Now, the cold "Atlantic sea" of materialism bars them apart.
  2. **"The new-born fire stood before the starry king":** Here are the two ultimate Contraries of *The Marriage of Heaven and Hell* standing face-to-face!
     * **The New-Born Fire:** Youthful, passionate, revolutionary **Energy** (Hell / Desire / Orc).
     * **The Starry King:** Aged, cold, authoritarian **Reason** (Heaven / Law / Empire / whom Blake soon names **Urizen**). Why is he called **"the starry king"**? Because in the eighteenth century, the distant, mathematically predictable stars were the ultimate symbol of Isaac Newton’s mechanical clockwork universe, cold fate ("Destiny"), and remote royal tyranny.

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  Long ago in ancient legends, before the Atlantic Ocean separated Europe and America, there were shining **"infinite mountains of light"** (like the lost island of Atlantis).
  Right on top of those glowing mountains, the brand-new baby—who is already standing on his feet and glowing like bright flames (**"the new-born fire"**)—stands bravely face-to-face in front of a grumpy, icy old ruler called **"the starry king"**!
  * **The New-Born Fire** is **Young Creative Energy and Freedom**!
  * **The Starry King** is **Old Bossy Rules** (who rules over the cold, dark night sky like a strict clockwork boss)!

---

### Verse 9

> **Original Text:**
> "9. Flagg’d with grey-brow’d snows and thunderous visages, the jealous wings wav’d over the deep."

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* 9. Weighed down ("Flagg’d") with hoary, grey-browed winter snows and surrounded by scowling, thunderous faces, the Starry King’s wings of possessive jealousy hovered menacingly over the abyss.
  *Implied Meaning:* Look at the sensory contrast between the two opponents: while the newborn child is pure, warm **Fire**, the Starry King is freezing **Winter** ("grey-brow’d snows"), gloomy intimidation ("thunderous visages"—recall the "sullen frowning fool" of Proverb 32), and **Jealousy** ("the jealous wings"). In the Old Testament (Exodus 20:5), the authoritarian Lawgiver declares, *"I the Lord thy God am a jealous God."* For Blake, "jealousy" is the defining vice of the tyrant (and the Devourer): the possessive fear of losing control over others' freedom and joy.

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  Look at what the grumpy **Starry King** looks like: his eyebrows are covered in freezing **winter snow and ice** ("grey-brow’d snows"), he is surrounded by angry, frowning faces ("thunderous visages"), and he flaps his giant **"jealous wings"** over the deep ocean! Why is the old king so jealous? Because he wants to be the only boss in the universe, and he is jealous of the bright, warm **New-Born Fire**!

---

### Verse 10

> **Original Text:**
> "10. The speary hand burn’d aloft; unbuckled was the shield; forth went the hand of jealousy among the flaming hair, and hurl’d the new-born wonder through the starry night."

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* 10. The king’s spear-wielding hand blazed high in the air and his defensive shield was unbuckled for battle; then his jealous hand reached out, seized the child by his locks of flaming hair, and hurled the "new-born wonder" headlong down through the starry night sky!
  *Implied Meaning:* This verse reenacts and subverts the myth of the **Fall of Satan/Vulcan** in John Milton’s *Paradise Lost* (and Zeus hurling Hephaestus from Mount Olympus). Recall Section 2 (*The Voice of the Devil*): *"It indeed appeared to Reason as if desire was cast out..."* Threatened by the birth of living Energy ("the new-born wonder"), the jealous tyrant drops his defensive posture ("unbuckled was the shield"), grabs the fiery youth by his "flaming hair," and casts him out of the heavens into the night!

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  The jealous Starry King raises his sharp spear high in the air and unbuckles his heavy shield! Then he reaches out his jealous hand, grabs the glowing child right by his **flaming hair**, and throws **"the new-born wonder"** flying across the starry night sky!
  Notice how Blake changed the child's nickname from *"new-born terror"* to **"new-born wonder"**—because to us, creative freedom isn't scary at all; it is a wonderful miracle!

---

### Verse 11

> **Original Text:**
> "11. The fire, the fire is falling!"

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* 11. Behold! The living fire—the sacred flame of human liberty—is falling from the sky toward the earth!
  *Implied Meaning:* Like Prometheus bringing fire down from heaven to humanity, or like Jesus’s words in Luke 12:49 (*"I am come to send fire on the earth"* and *"I beheld Satan as lightning fall from heaven"* in Luke 10:18), the fall of the fiery child is not a defeat—it is the arrival of revolutionary Energy upon the earth to awaken sleeping humanity!

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  A voice cries out across the sky: **"The fire, the fire is falling!"**
  Like a giant, glowing shooting star or comet of freedom, the fiery spirit is zooming down from the sky toward Earth to wake everyone up!

### Verse 12

> **Original Text:**
> "12. Look up! look up! O citizen of London, enlarge thy countenance! O Jew, leave counting gold; return to thy oil and wine! O African, black African! (Go, winged thought, widen his forehead.)"

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* 12. Look up at the descending fire of liberty! Look up! O citizen of London, expand your narrow, careworn face and perception! O Jewish brother, lay aside the commercial money-counting forced upon you by modern Europe and return to your ancient prophetic abundance of sacred oil and joyful wine! O enslaved Black African! (Fly forth, O winged thought of universal liberty, break his chains, and expand his brow in freedom and dignity!)
  *Implied Meaning:* As the fire of liberty streaks across the sky (echoing Proverb 54: *"Lift up thy head!"*), Blake issues a thrilling call for **universal human emancipation** across three groups trapped by eighteenth-century European oppression:
  1. **The Citizen of London:** Trapped in the narrow "cavern" of industrial materialism and commercial anxiety (which Blake soon immortalizes in his poem *"London"*), the Londoner is urged to **"enlarge thy countenance"**—to open his eyes and expand his contracted senses.
  2. **The Jewish People:** In medieval and eighteenth-century Europe, discriminatory laws barred Jewish people from owning land or entering most professions, forcing many into urban banking and commerce ("counting gold"). Remembering Ezekiel’s speech at dinner in Section 6—where Israel was celebrated as the home of King David and the **Poetic Genius**—Blake calls upon the Jewish people to leave behind modern commercial materialism and return to the joyful, sensual, and prophetic abundance of biblical **"oil and wine"** (Psalm 104:15: *"wine that maketh glad the heart of man, and oil to make his face to shine"*).
  3. **The Black African:** Writing at the height of the British abolitionist movement against the transatlantic slave trade (around 1790–1792, when Blake was also illustrating John Gabriel Stedman’s anti-slavery book and writing *Visions of the Daughters of Albion*), Blake sends out **"winged thought"** to liberate enslaved Africans and dismantle the racist lie that denied their intellectual and spiritual equality ("widen his forehead"—in eighteenth-century portraiture, a wide, open brow symbolized noble intellect and free humanity).

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  As that bright shooting star of Freedom flies across the sky, Blake shouts to people all over the world: **"Look up! Look up!"**
  * First, he calls to his neighbors in **London**: *"Stop staring down at the ground with worried, pinched faces—open your eyes and smile wide ('enlarge thy countenance')!"*
  * Next, he calls to the **Jewish people**: *"Stop worrying about counting gold coins in banks—go back to the joyful celebrations, music, and poetry of your ancient prophets ('return to thy oil and wine')!"*
  * And third, he calls out with deep love to the **Black African** people (who were being unfairly captured and enslaved in Blake's time): *"Go, flying thought of Freedom ('winged thought'), set them free and let their minds and dreams shine wide and tall ('widen his forehead')!"* Blake believed that **every single person of every nation and skin color** deserves to be completely free!

### Verse 13

> **Original Text:**
> "13. The fiery limbs, the flaming hair shot like the sinking sun into the Western sea."

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* 13. The child’s blazing limbs and locks of flaming hair plunged like the setting sun straight into the western Atlantic Ocean.
  *Implied Meaning:* Hurled downward by the Starry King, the fiery spirit shoots westward like a blazing sunset into **"the Western sea"** (the Atlantic Ocean, towards America—the land of revolution and the West, which in Blake’s symbolic compass represents the realm of the body and new political liberty).

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  With his arms and legs glowing like fire and his flaming hair streaming behind him, the fiery child zooms across the sky just like the giant red sun setting in the evening, splashing right down into the **Western Sea** (the Atlantic Ocean)!

---

### Verse 14

> **Original Text:**
> "14. Wak’d from his eternal sleep, the hoary element roaring fled away."

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* 14. Jolted awake from its ancient, primordial slumber by the impact of the living fire, the white-foamed, aged ocean ("the hoary element") roared aloud and fled back in clouds of steam!
  *Implied Meaning:* "Hoary" means white-haired with extreme age (referring to the white foam of the ancient ocean waves). Just as the sea fled in clouds of smoke before the fiery eyes of Leviathan in Section 10 (and as the Red Sea parted in Exodus 14, or as Revelation 21:1 prophesies that *"there was no more sea"*), the impact of the New-Born Fire boils away the cold ocean barrier ("the hoary element") that had kept humanity divided and asleep!

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  **"Hoary"** means having white hair like a very old grandfather—which is Blake’s poetic way of describing the white, foamy waves of the ancient ocean ("the hoary element").
  When that blazing ball of Fire splashes into the cold ocean, it wakes the sleepy old ocean right up! With a giant *SIZZLE* and a loud roar, the ocean waves roll back and flee away!

### Verse 15

> **Original Text:**
> "15. Down rush’d, beating his wings in vain, the jealous king, his grey-brow’d councillors, thunderous warriors, curl’d veterans, among helms and shields, and chariots, horses, elephants, banners, castles, slings, and rocks."

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* 15. Suddenly, dragged down by the collapse of his own empire, the Jealous King himself plunged headlong from the sky, flapping his wings uselessly ("in vain"), accompanied by his aged, grey-browed advisers, his scowling soldiers, and his aristocratic, curled-wigged military veterans—all tumbling in a chaotic avalanche of helmets, shields, war-chariots, war-horses, battle-elephants, royal flags, stone castles, slings, and boulders!
  *Implied Meaning:* Look back at Section 2 (*The Voice of the Devil*): *"It indeed appeared to Reason as if desire was cast out, but the Devil’s account is, that **the Messiah [Reason/the Governor] fell**..."*
  Here Blake dramatizes that exact reversal! In throwing the New-Born Fire down, the Jealous King has destabilized his own tyranny and falls from the sky alongside his entire military-feudal apparatus! Notice the satirical detail **"curl’d veterans"**—mocking eighteenth-century European generals and aristocrats in their powdered, curled wigs alongside ancient imperial war machines (chariots, elephants, castles) and Pharaoh’s army drowning in the Red Sea (Exodus 14:28). Flapping his jealous wings "in vain," the tyrant’s military power collapses under its own weight.

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  Look what happens next! By throwing the fiery child down and making the ocean flee away, the grumpy **Jealous King** accidentally pulls the rug out from under his own feet!
  Flapping his wings helplessly ("in vain"), the Jealous King comes **tumbling down** out of the sky too! And falling right behind him is his whole bossy army: his grumpy old advisers ("grey-brow’d councillors"), his loud soldiers ("thunderous warriors"), his fancy generals wearing curled wigs ("curl’d veterans"), along with all their helmets, shields, chariots, war-horses, elephants, flags, stone castles, slingshots, and rocks—all crashing down in one giant pile!

---

### Verse 16

> **Original Text:**
> "16. Falling, rushing, ruining; buried in the ruins, on Urthona’s dens."

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* 16. Tumbling, hurtling, and crashing into total ruin, the tyrant and his armies are buried beneath the rubble of their own collapsed castles upon the subterranean caverns of Urthona ("Urthona’s dens").
  *Implied Meaning:* The rhythmic alliteration (*"Falling, rushing, ruining"*) mimics the deafening crash of falling empires. Moreover, this verse marks the **very first appearance in all of Blake’s writings of the name "Urthona"**! In Blake’s mythological system, **Urthona** (whose name sounds like *"Earth-Owner"*) is the deep, subterranean spirit of the creative imagination—the cosmic blacksmith who works in the dark caverns ("dens") of the earth and the unconscious mind. The arrogant Starry King falls from his high sky-throne and is buried in the ruins of his own military empire right on top of the deep earth-caverns of Urthona.

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  *Crash! Boom! Rumble!* (**"Falling, rushing, ruining!"**) All the king’s stone castles and war machines smash into pieces, burying the grumpy king and his army underneath the rubble right on top of **"Urthona’s dens"** (which is Blake’s special name for the deep, underground caves of the Earth where the blacksmith of Imagination works)!

---

### Verse 17

> **Original Text:**
> "17. All night beneath the ruins; then their sullen flames, faded, emerge round the gloomy king."

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* 17. For the entire night, the fallen tyrant and his hosts lie pinned beneath the wreckage; finally, their joyless, smoky fires ("sullen flames"), dim and faded, creep out from the rubble around the "gloomy king."
  *Implied Meaning:* Blake brilliantly parodies Book I of John Milton’s *Paradise Lost*, where Satan and his fallen angels lie stunned for nine days on the burning lake of Hell, surrounded by "darkness visible" and lurid, joyless flames, before slowly raising their heads. In Blake’s version, however, the figure lying stunned amidst **"sullen flames, faded"** is *not* the fiery spirit of Desire, but **"the gloomy king"** of authoritarian Reason! While true Energy burns with bright, living fire, the defeated tyrant possesses only "sullen, faded" flames of bitter resentment.

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  All night long, the defeated king and his army lie stuck underneath the pile of broken rocks. Finally, as the night goes on, dim, smoky, grumpy little fires ("sullen flames, faded") flicker out of the rubble around **"the gloomy king."**

---

### Verse 18

> **Original Text:**
> "18. With thunder and fire, leading his starry hosts through the waste wilderness, he promulgates his ten commandments, glancing his beamy eyelids over the deep in dark dismay."

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* 18. Realizing that brute military force has failed him, the Gloomy King resorts to his ultimate weapon—religious fear and moral law: amidst intimidating thunder and lightning, leading his remaining starry armies through the barren desert ("the waste wilderness"), he officially proclaims ("promulgates") his legal code of the **Ten Commandments**, peering out from beneath his glaring, beam-like eyelids across the abyss in dark terror ("dark dismay").
  *Implied Meaning:* This is one of the most profound historical and psychological insights in Blake’s mythology ( echoing Section 5, *The Ancient Poets*, where Priesthood replaced military conquest). When a tyrant’s physical armies, chariots, and castles are defeated (Verses 15–17), how does he try to regain control over humanity? **By inventing dogmatic Religion and Moral Guilt!**
  Like an authoritarian lawgiver leading his hosts through the "waste wilderness" of Sinai amidst terrifying thunder and smoke (Exodus 19–20), the Gloomy King **"promulgates his ten commandments"** (attempting to chain human minds with *"Thou shalt not"*). Yet look at his eyes: he glances over the deep **"in dark dismay"**—because deep down, the tyrant is terrified that humanity is waking up and will no longer obey his stony laws!

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  Now that all his castles, chariots, and armies are broken, how does the **Gloomy King** try to stay the boss? He tries to use **scary, strict rules**!
  Marching his starry followers through a dry, empty desert ("the waste wilderness") with loud thunder and smoke, he pulls out heavy stone tablets and announces his strict rulebook—**"his ten commandments"**—peeking out with his glaring eyes across the ocean in secret fear (**"in dark dismay"**) because he is scared that nobody is going to fall for his bossy rules anymore!

---

### Verse 19

> **Original Text:**
> "19. Where the Son of Fire in his Eastern cloud, while the Morning plumes her golden breast,"

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* 19. And why is the Gloomy King filled with such dark dismay? Because looking toward the East, he beholds **"the Son of Fire"** rising in a glorious eastern cloud of dawn, just as the Morning preens her golden feathers like an awakening bird ("plumes her golden breast"),
  *Implied Meaning:* Remember in Verse 13 that the fiery child shot like the *setting sun* into the *Western* sea? Having traveled beneath the earth through the night, he now rises triumphantly in the **East** as the **Morning Sun** of a new spiritual dawn! Blake’s lyrical metaphor—*"while the Morning plumes her golden breast"*—likens the breaking dawn to a golden bird smoothing its feathers and preparing to soar (recalling the bird that is "an immense world of delight" and the Eagle of Genius).

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  Why is the Gloomy King so scared as he looks across the sky?
  Because over in the East, the long dark night is finally over, and the **Morning** is waking up like a beautiful golden bird fluffing her bright feathers (**"while the Morning plumes her golden breast"**)! And rising right up inside a glowing eastern cloud is that brave fiery child—now grown into **"the Son of Fire"**—shining like the brand-new morning sun!

### Verse 20

> **Original Text:**
> "20. Spurning the clouds written with curses, stamps the stony law to dust, loosing the eternal horses from the dens of night, crying: “Empire is no more! and now the lion and wolf shall cease.”"

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* 20. Kicking aside with disdain ("Spurning") the dark storm-clouds inscribed with the tyrant’s religious curses, the Son of Fire crushes the stone tablets of repressive law into powder ("stamps the stony law to dust"), unchains the immortal horses of instruction and energy from the dark caverns of night, and proclaims aloud to the universe: **"Empire is no more! and now the lion and wolf shall cease."**
  *Implied Meaning:* Every phrase of this climactic twentieth verse resolves a major theme from earlier in *The Marriage of Heaven and Hell*:
  1. **"Spurning the clouds written with curses":** In Section 12, the orthodox Angel sat on a "cloud," and in Proverb 55, the priest laid his "curse on the fairest joys." The risen Son of Fire kicks away the gloomy clouds of priestly guilt and damnation.
  2. **"Stamps the stony law to dust":** Just as the Devil in Section 12 proved that Jesus acted *"from impulse, not from rules"* and broke the literal Ten Commandments, the Son of Fire shatters the cold stone tablets of legalistic oppression ("the stony law" / "stones of law" from Proverb 21) into harmless dust!
  3. **"Loosing the eternal horses from the dens of night":** Recall Proverb 44: *"The tigers of wrath are wiser than the horses of instruction."* Under the Gloomy King’s rule, the noble horses of the mind were bridled and locked in "the dens of night"; now they are set free as **"eternal horses"** of the sun chariot!
  4. **"Crying: 'Empire is no more! and now the lion and wolf shall cease'":** First, all political and spiritual tyranny (**"Empire"**) is abolished. Second, once oppressive Empire ceases, even the fierce, defensive wrath of the **lion** and the ravenous hunger of the **wolf** (which were necessary to fight tyranny in a fallen world, as in *The Argument* and Proverb 27) are no longer needed and can rest in universal peace—fulfilling the prophecy of Isaiah 11:6 and 35:9 (the very chapter Blake cited in *The Argument*!).

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  Look at what the **Son of Fire** does as he rises in the golden morning sunshine:
  1. First, he kicks away ("spurns") the dark, grumpy storm clouds that were covered in mean curses!
  2. Next, he takes the Gloomy King’s heavy stone tablets of unfair rules and **stomps that stony law right into dust** so it can never hurt anyone again!
  3. Then, he opens the dark cages ("the dens of night") and sets the **"eternal horses"** free so they can run wildly across the morning sky!
  4. Finally, he shouts out the happiest news in the universe: **"Empire is no more! And now the lion and wolf shall cease!"** That means there are no more bully kings or unfair empires, and because everyone is finally safe and free, even the fierce lions and wolves don't have to fight or roar anymore—everyone can live together in peace!

## The Concluding Chorus

*(Note on the Chorus: Following the twenty numbered verses of "A Song of Liberty," Blake concludes the entire book with a four-sentence **Chorus** where all awakened humanity sings together in celebration of political, spiritual, and bodily freedom.)*

### Chorus – Sentence 1

> **Original Text:**
> "Let the Priests of the Raven of Dawn, no longer in deadly black, with hoarse note curse the Sons of Joy."

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* Let the black-robed clergy—who act like croaking ravens spoiling the break of dawn—no longer wear their vestments of death ("deadly black") or use their harsh, rasping voices ("with hoarse note") to pronounce religious curses upon joyful, creative human beings ("the Sons of Joy").
  *Implied Meaning:* Throughout the *Proverbs of Hell*, Blake associated orthodox priests with scavenging black birds (Proverb 39: *"the crow"*; Proverb 62: *"The crow wished everything was black"*) and with cursing human happiness (Proverb 55: *"so the priest lays his curse on the fairest joys"*). Dressed in their traditional "deadly black" cassocks and robes, authoritarian priests greet every new dawn of human joy with a funeral croak ("hoarse note") of guilt and sin. The Chorus proclaims that their reign over **"the Sons of Joy"** (liberated humanity living in "Eternal Delight") is over forever!

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  Now everyone joins together to sing the final **Chorus**!
  First, they sing that grumpy rule-makers ("Priests")—who used to dress all in **"deadly black"** feathers like a croaking black **raven** and squawk with scratchy voices ("hoarse note") to spoil everyone's morning—must never again put grumpy rules or curses on happy, creative people (**"the Sons of Joy"**)!

---

### Chorus – Sentence 2

> **Original Text:**
> "Nor his accepted brethren whom, tyrant, he calls free, lay the bound or build the roof."

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* Nor let the tyrant’s favored, respectable citizens ("his accepted brethren")—whom the tyrant flatteringly calls "free men" because they obey his system—ever again draw restrictive boundaries ("lay the bound") around human energy or construct a closed ceiling ("build the roof") to shut out the infinite sky!
  *Implied Meaning:* Blake exposes the illusion of conventional "freedom" inside an authoritarian society. A tyrant (or dogmatic church) always has a privileged class of compliant, respectable followers (**"his accepted brethren"**—like the "Angels" and the "Devourers") whom the tyrant praises as "free citizens," even though they are merely well-behaved servants of the system! And what do these conformists do? They **"lay the bound"** (turn Reason’s circumference into a prison wall) and **"build the roof"** (close off the top of the human cavern so that no one can look up at the infinite sky or soar like the Eagle!).

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  Next, the Chorus sings that the grumpy king’s favorite helpers ("his accepted brethren")—who think they are "free" just because they are the king’s pets—are no longer allowed to **build walls ("lay the bound")** around people's imaginations or **build a heavy roof ("build the roof")** over our heads to block out the endless blue sky!

---

### Chorus – Sentence 3

> **Original Text:**
> "Nor pale religious lechery call that virginity that wishes, but acts not!"

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* And never again let sickly, repressed religious hypocrisy ("pale religious lechery") dare to give the pure name of "virginity" (innocence and holiness) to a state of mind that secretly harbors desires ("that wishes") while cowardly refusing to act upon them ("but acts not")!
  *Implied Meaning:* Capping the psychological critique he began in Proverb 5 (*"He who desires, but acts not, breeds pestilence"*), Proverb 21 (*"brothels with bricks of religion"*), and Proverb 66 (*"Sooner murder an infant in its cradle than nurse unacted desires"*), Blake unmasks puritanical asceticism. When orthodox moralists suppress their natural human love and vitality, they grow **"pale"** with repression and inwardly obsessed with the very desires they condemn (**"religious lechery"**—corrupt, joyless obsession born of repression). Yet they hypocritically call their frustrated inaction "purity" or "virginity"! For Blake, true innocence ("the soul of sweet delight" that "can never be defiled" in Proverb 53) is honest, open-hearted, active love—never the festering repression that *"wishes, but acts not!"*

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  Remember Proverb 5 from earlier: *"He who desires, but acts not, breeds pestilence"*?
  Here the Chorus sings that nobody should ever pretend they are being "extra pure and good" just because they sit still on the outside while secretly wishing for things on the inside (**"that wishes, but acts not"**)! Real goodness isn't hiding in a dark room afraid to move—real goodness is stepping out into the sunshine and acting with an honest, joyful heart!

---

### Chorus – Sentence 4

> **Original Text:**
> "For everything that lives is holy."

* **Sentence Translation and Detailed Implied Meaning (For Adults):**
  *Translation:* **For every single living being, energy, passion, body, and spirit in existence is sacred ("holy")!**
  *Implied Meaning:* With these six immortal words—**"For everything that lives is holy"**—William Blake brings *The Marriage of Heaven and Hell* to a triumphant close.
  Look back across the entire journey of the book:
  * Traditional dualistic religion split the universe in two, calling the Soul, Reason, and passive obedience "holy," while condemning the Body, Energy, passion, and nature as "corrupt" and "unholy."
  * Through the *Voice of the Devil*, the *Proverbs of Hell*, the *Memorable Fancies*, and *A Song of Liberty*, Blake has melted away that false division with the "corroding fires" of his art, cleansing the doors of perception so that Heaven (Reason/Form) and Hell (Energy/Life) are finally united in a true **Marriage**.
  Nothing that genuinely *lives*—not the roaring lion, the soaring eagle, the proud peacock, the cut worm, the little flower, the human body, or the blazing fire of imagination—is excluded from the Divine. Because *"God only acts and is in existing beings or men,"* **everything that lives is holy.**

* **Detailed Explanation (For an 8-Year-Old):**
  And here, at the very end of the whole book, William Blake leaves us with the six best, brightest, and most important words of all:
  > **"For everything that lives is holy."**
  What does **"holy"** mean? It means sacred, precious, wonderful, and filled with God's own light!
  Not just churches or rulebooks—**EVERYTHING that lives is holy!**
  * The little bird flying across the sky is holy!
  * The proud peacock, the roaring lion, the gentle lamb, and the tiny flower in the grass are holy!
  * Your body, your five senses, your big feelings, your careful thinking (**Reason**), and your bright, fiery imagination (**Energy**) are ALL **holy**!
  When you remember that *everything that lives is holy*, you treat yourself, other people, animals, and the whole living world with wonder, respect, and joy!

## Comprehensive Summaries of Section 13: "A Song of Liberty and Chorus"

### 1. Detailed Summary for Adults and Educators

In **Section 13 (Plates 25–27)**, William Blake concludes *The Marriage of Heaven and Hell* with **"A Song of Liberty"** and its **"Chorus,"** uniting his historical celebration of eighteenth-century revolution with a cosmic myth of human emancipation:

* **The Global Wave of Revolution (Verses 1–6):** The "Eternal Female" (Mother Nature / collective humanity) groans in labor across the earth. As Britain ("Albion") lies in fearful silence and post-revolutionary America catches its breath, the prophetic spirit crosses the Atlantic, calling on **France** to tear down the Bastille (*"rend down thy dungeon!"*), **Spain** to shatter the barriers of the Inquisition, and **Rome** to cast its papal keys of spiritual control into the deep forever.
* **The Clash of the New-Born Fire and the Starry King (Verses 7–17):** The Eternal Female gives birth to the howling **"new-born terror / wonder"** of revolutionary Energy (later named **Orc**), who confronts the wintry, jealous **"starry king"** of oppressive Reason and Empire (later named **Urizen**) upon the lost Atlantean mountains of light. When the jealous king hurls the fiery youth down into the Western sea—while Blake calls upon the citizen of London, the Jewish people, and enslaved Black Africans to look up and claim universal freedom—the impact wakes the ancient ocean and drags the jealous king and his entire military empire crashing down in ruins onto **Urthona’s dens**.
* **The Shattering of the Stony Law and the Final Chorus (Verses 18–20 & Chorus):** Emerging amidst faded flames, the defeated king attempts to re-enslave humanity in the wilderness by promulgating the **Ten Commandments**. However, rising in the golden eastern dawn, the **"Son of Fire"** spurns the clouds of curses, **"stamps the stony law to dust,"** frees the eternal horses of the mind, and proclaims the end of Empire and predatory strife. The concluding **Chorus** banishes the black-robed priests of guilt, forbids conformists from building walls or roofs against the infinite, exposes the hypocrisy of repressed inaction (*"that wishes, but acts not"*), and crowns the entire book with the ultimate affirmation of spiritual monism: **"For everything that lives is holy."**

### 2. Detailed Summary for an 8-Year-Old Learner

In **Section 13: A Song of Liberty and Chorus**, William Blake finishes his book with an exciting song about how **Freedom and Joy** win over unfair bosses all around the world:

1. **The Birth of the Fiery Spirit of Freedom:** Mother Earth groans and gives birth to a glowing, brave baby called **"the new-born fire"** and **"the new-born wonder"** (the Spirit of Freedom!). As this fiery spirit flies across the sky—telling France to tear down unfair prisons and calling out to people in London, Jewish people, and Black Africans that *everyone* deserves to be free—a jealous, icy old ruler called **"the starry king"** throws the fiery child down into the Western ocean!
2. **Stomping Unfair Rules to Dust:** When the fiery child splashes into the ocean, the grumpy Starry King and all his castles, chariots, and armies come crashing down into the rubble! Even when the gloomy king tries to use heavy stone tablets of strict rules to scare people, the **Son of Fire** rises up like the bright golden **Morning Sun**, kicks away the grumpy clouds, **stomps the stony rules into dust**, sets the horses free, and shouts: **"Empire is no more! And now the lion and wolf shall cease!"**
3. **"For Everything That Lives Is Holy":** At the very end, everyone sings the **Chorus** together: no more grumpy black ravens spoiling our joy, no more roofs blocking out the endless sky, and no more hiding our good ideas instead of acting on them—because **"everything that lives is holy!"** Every living creature, every person, our minds, our bodies, and our imaginations are sacred and full of wonder!
